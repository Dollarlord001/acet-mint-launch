# Mint Foundation Launch

Build an exceptionally polished, premium launch/waitlist website for ACET CONSULTS, an emerging technology and professional opportunity platform launching in October 2026.



This is NOT a generic SaaS landing page. Treat it like a high-end technology brand launch campaign.



The visual identity must be built around MINT GREEN + BLACK + WHITE.



These are the official ACET colours for now. Do not introduce unrelated brand colours. Additional colours may be added to the brand system later.



---



1. CORE BRAND



Brand:

ACET CONSULTS



Launch:

October 2026



Core positioning:



ACET is building a smarter ecosystem where people can discover opportunities, connect with businesses and professionals, use AI-powered assistance, and make better-informed decisions through trust and verification.



Primary brand statement:



OPPORTUNITY IS EVERYWHERE.

TRUST SHOULDN'T BE.



The website should immediately communicate:



- Technology

- Intelligence

- Opportunity

- Trust

- Professionalism

- Future-focused thinking



---



2. VISUAL DIRECTION



The overall aesthetic should feel:



Futuristic + cinematic + intelligent + editorial + premium + human



Think of a serious technology company preparing for a major product launch.



Do NOT make it look like:



- a generic AI template

- a crypto website

- a gaming website

- a children's website

- a basic startup template

- an overly-glowing cyberpunk website



Use restraint.



The design should feel expensive because of its composition, typography, spacing, photography and motion — NOT because everything is glowing.



---



3. OFFICIAL COLOUR SYSTEM



Use ONLY this primary brand system:



MINT GREEN



Primary accent.



Use it for:



- important highlights

- buttons

- active states

- small interface indicators

- subtle glows

- data lines

- verification indicators

- selected words

- interactive elements



BLACK



Primary background and visual foundation.



Use multiple shades of near-black to create depth rather than making every section identical.



WHITE



Primary typography and high-contrast elements.



Secondary text may use controlled shades of white/gray, but keep the overall palette disciplined.



IMPORTANT:



Do NOT introduce purple, pink, orange, red, cyan, blue or random gradient colours.



Mint green should be the signature visual cue throughout the entire experience.



---



4. HERO — THE MAIN VISUAL EXPERIENCE



Create a full-screen cinematic hero.



I will upload TWO IMPORTANT VISUAL ASSETS:



1. A person wearing a VR headset

2. A chart/data visual that I want to use as part of the background



USE THESE UPLOADED ASSETS.



Do not replace them with stock imagery.



The person wearing the VR headset should become the human focal point of the hero.



The VR headset person should communicate:



seeing beyond the obvious / entering a new world of opportunity / intelligent technology / the future of work.



Composition:



Place the VR subject prominently but elegantly.



Do not simply put the image inside a rectangular card.



Integrate the person into the environment using:



- cinematic cropping

- soft edge blending where appropriate

- subtle lighting

- depth

- controlled shadows

- atmospheric overlays



The chart should exist behind or around the subject as a living data environment.



---



5. ANIMATED CHART BACKGROUND



The uploaded chart must be used as a major visual layer.



Make the chart subtly move.



The animation should feel like a sophisticated live data system.



Use:



- extremely slow horizontal movement

- subtle vertical drift

- very slight scale/parallax

- gentle opacity transitions

- slow data-line movement

- layered depth



The movement should be almost subconscious.



The user should feel:



"Something is happening behind this interface."



NOT:



"A picture is moving around."



Use GPU-friendly CSS transforms wherever possible.



Do not use expensive WebGL or unnecessary libraries.



Add a subtle mint-green treatment to parts of the chart only if it can be done without destroying the original asset.



Do not distort the uploaded chart.



---



6. HERO TYPOGRAPHY



Large, powerful headline:



OPPORTUNITY IS EVERYWHERE.

TRUST SHOULDN'T BE.



Use typography with serious visual presence.



Make TRUST or TRUST SHOULDN'T BE the strongest visual emphasis using ACET mint green.



Supporting copy:



We're building a smarter platform for discovering opportunities, connecting professionals and businesses, and making better decisions with AI-powered assistance and verification.



Small eyebrow:



ACET CONSULTS / LAUNCHING OCTOBER 2026



Primary CTA:



JOIN THE WAITLIST →



Secondary CTA:



DISCOVER ACET



Add a small animated mint-green indicator:



SYSTEM STATUS · BUILDING



---



7. HERO MICRO-INTERACTIONS



When the page loads:



1. Background appears first.

2. VR subject subtly reveals.

3. Chart begins its slow movement.

4. Eyebrow fades upward.

5. Headline reveals line-by-line.

6. Supporting text appears.

7. CTA buttons appear.

8. Small system indicator activates.



Keep the entire sequence elegant and fast.



Do not create a long intro animation that prevents users from accessing the page.



---



8. SCROLL EXPERIENCE



The website should feel like the user is moving through an evolving digital environment.



As they scroll:



- the chart environment subtly changes position

- sections transition smoothly

- typography reveals progressively

- mint-green data lines occasionally connect sections

- images have subtle depth movement

- important statements become visually dominant



Do NOT animate everything.



Motion must communicate meaning.

9. THE PROBLEM



Create a dramatic editorial section.



Headline:



THE INTERNET CREATED MORE OPPORTUNITIES.

IT DIDN'T MAKE THEM EASIER TO TRUST.



Explain:



Opportunities are scattered across countless platforms.



People often have limited context about who is behind an opportunity.



Businesses and professionals need better signals before investing their time.



Use three visually sophisticated statements:



NOISE



Too many opportunities. Too little signal.



UNCERTAINTY



Not enough context about who you're dealing with.



TRUST



The missing layer between discovery and action.



Use mint-green typography accents and minimal data-style visual elements.

CONNECT

GROW

Imagine a minimal digital network where each stage connects to the next using thin mint-green lines.



Animate the connections as the user scrolls.

18. TYPOGRAPHY



Use a premium display typeface for major headings.



A style similar to:



Bebas Neue / condensed editorial display



can be used for major statements.



Use a clean modern sans-serif for body text and interface elements.



The typography should create strong contrast between:



DISPLAY

and

INFORMATION.



Avoid excessive font weights.



Large headlines should have generous line spacing and intentional breaks.



---



19. RESPONSIVE DESIGN



This website MUST be designed mobile-first.



I am primarily using Android.



Test approximately:



360px

390px

412px

768px

1024px

1440px



On mobile:



- VR subject must remain visually dominant

- chart must not make text unreadable

- headline must remain powerful

- CTA must be easy to tap

- no horizontal scrolling

- images must scale intelligently

- animations must remain smooth

- waitlist form must be extremely easy to complete



---



20. PERFORMANCE



The final site will be deployed to Netlify.



Keep it lightweight.



- Optimise uploaded images.

- Lazy-load below-the-fold images.

- Use CSS animations whenever possible.

- Avoid unnecessary animation frameworks.

- Avoid WebGL.

- Avoid heavy 3D libraries.

- Avoid unnecessary dependencies.

- Avoid autoplay video.

- Use GPU-friendly transforms.

- Respect "prefers-reduced-motion".

- Keep JavaScript minimal.



The cinematic effect must NOT come at the expense of performance.



---



21. ACCESSIBILITY



Use:



- semantic HTML

- proper heading hierarchy

- keyboard-accessible controls

- readable contrast

- descriptive alt text

- accessible form labels

- visible focus states

- reduced-motion support



---



22. IMPORTANT AUTHENTICITY RULES



Do NOT invent:



- customers

- testimonials

- statistics

- partnerships

- awards

- company logos

- user numbers

- revenue figures

- fake product screenshots presented as real

- fake verification results



If something is a concept, make it visually clear that it is a concept.



ACET is preparing for launch.



The website should create excitement without pretending the product already has things it doesn't.



---



23. FINAL DESIGN TEST



Before considering the project complete, ask:



Does this look like a serious technology company preparing to launch a major product?



Does the mint-green / black / white identity immediately feel recognisable?



Does the VR subject feel integrated into the brand story?



Does the animated chart feel like a living data environment?



Does the first screen communicate ACET within five seconds?



Does the page feel premium without becoming visually noisy?



Does the waitlist feel like the natural next action?



Does everything work beautifully on Android?



If any answer is no, refine the design before finishing.



The final result should feel like:



ACET CONSULTS — a futuristic, intelligent, trustworthy technology brand entering its next chapter.
